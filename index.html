// KNOWLEDGE VAULT

ABOUT & FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to essential queries regarding team size, entry fees, payment validation, problem statements, and campus logistics.

Teams must consist strictly of 3 or 4 members. The registration fee is ₹349 per member:

  • 3 Members: ₹349 × 3 = ₹1,047 total
  • 4 Members: ₹349 × 4 = ₹1,396 total

This fee covers round-the-clock campus security, high-speed Wi-Fi, official hacker kits, 5 gourmet meals (High Tea, Dinner, Midnight Fuel, Day 2 Breakfast, Lunch), and certificates.

In accordance with hackathon guidelines, problem statements for each of the 6 Infinity Domains will be unlocked just an hour before the start of the event. Team leaders can log in to their dashboard at any time to view countdowns and select their problem statement once unlocked by organizers.

During registration, pay the exact fee via UPI to infinityhack@okaxis or scan the dynamic QR code. Upload your payment screenshot. Our portal automatically scans the image to identify your 12-digit UTR number and phone number.

Note: Every transaction UTR must be completely unique. However, the same phone number can be used if a college coordinator or faculty mentor is paying on behalf of multiple squads.

Teams undergo 3 milestone reviews with industry judges. To preserve competitive suspense and prevent bias, judges' numerical scores and evaluation rubrics remain strictly confidential and hidden from team leader dashboards. Final rankings are unveiled during the Grand Valedictory Ceremony.
